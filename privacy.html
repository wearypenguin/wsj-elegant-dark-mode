Privacy Policy

WSJ Elegant Dark Mode is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data Collection

The extension runs entirely in your browser and only changes the visual appearance of The Wall Street Journal website by applying dark-themed styles. We do not:

  • Collect any personal data
  • Use analytics or tracking
  • Store browsing history
  • Access your WSJ subscription information
  • Modify, collect, or transmit article content
  • Use cookies for tracking purposes

Permissions

Any permissions requested by the extension are strictly for enabling dark mode functionality on The Wall Street Journal website. The extension does not bypass the paywall, alter content, or modify your subscription in any way.

Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties. There are no third-party services integrated into this extension.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page accordingly.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer at pamuszynski@gmail.com.
